A Chance to Die - Elisabeth Elliot (RM48)

Amy Charmichael bio. She went to India with a changed life. Good but, a bit pricey!

A Night To Remember - Walter Lord (RM49 - but in the public domain next year)

The sinking of the Titanic. Man, rich people are bad!

Submarine - Tom Clancy (RM32)

Non-fiction book about submarines. Geeky and cool!

Eternal Punishment - A. W. Pink

Hell. That's it. STELLAR. But, skip the first chapter, it stinks!

By This Standard - Greg Bahnsen

If you like to think, do yourself a favour, get this book! Bahnsen is excellent.

Famous Atheists - Ray Comfort (RM32)

Short but excellent overview of famous Atheists. Worth RM32, but unfortunately it used to be free.

Crucifixion - John Bowden (RM74)

The history of crucifixion.

Dark Journey Deep Grace - Roy Ratcliff (RM40)

The story of a dispicable man and how his life utterly changed. Worth every penny.

Death of a Guru - Rabi R Maharaja (RM40)

The true story of a Hindu priest, his family and, well, how that all ended.

The Defense of the Faith - Cornelius Van Til

Get an ice pack, your brain will need it. But WOW, jsut wow. (In the Public Domain in Malaysia)

Defying Jihad - Esther Ahmad (RM50)

Haven't read this yet, but have heard it is amazing.

Engaging the Culture - Ken Dew (RM40)

I know the author pretty well. Butt that doesn't matter - challenging book!
